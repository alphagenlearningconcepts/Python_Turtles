Randomness & RGB

Fun Challenge: random.randint() & Colorful Concentric Art

What is random.randint()?

random.randint(0, 255) picks a random whole number from 0 to 255. Because RGB colors mix Red, Green, and Blue (0–255), putting it inside your concentric loop changes the shade on every repeat!

import turtle, random
turtle.colormode(255)
for outer in range(12):
    r = random.randint(0, 255) # 255
    g = random.randint(0, 255) # 208
    b = random.randint(0, 255) # 113
    pen.pencolor(r, g, b)
    pen.fillcolor(r, g, b)
